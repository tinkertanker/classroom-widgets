Quieter volume levels for desktop widgets.

- Desktop: choose Mute or Levels 1–4 in Settings and floating-widget volume menus. Level 1 is quieter than the previous lowest preset; the levels use audio gains of 0.10, 0.25, 0.50 and 1.00, without percentage labels.
- Volume changes stay in sync across open widgets and Settings and are remembered for next time. Older non-preset volume settings appear as Custom and are kept until you choose a level.
- Windows: pressing Escape in the volume selector no longer accidentally selects and saves Mute.
- macOS: newly opened widget panels immediately show the current volume level in their tooltip and accessibility label.
