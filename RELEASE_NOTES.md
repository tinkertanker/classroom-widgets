Four clear volume levels for desktop widgets.

- Desktop: choose Mute or Levels 1–4 in Settings and floating-widget volume menus. The levels use audio gains of 0.10, 0.25, 0.50 and 1.00, without percentage labels.
- Volume changes stay in sync across open widgets and Settings and are remembered for next time. Older non-preset volume settings appear as Custom and are kept until you choose a level.
- Windows: Escape leaves the selected volume unchanged.
- macOS: newly opened widget panels immediately show the current volume level in their tooltip and accessibility label.
